| 阅读技巧 | All stories have characters. Characters are the people animals, or things that are in the story. There are main characters and secondary characters.<br>Main characters are the most important characters.The story is about them.<br>Secondary characters aren't as important as the main characters.<br>所有的故事都有角色。人物是人、动物或故事中的事物。有主要人物和次要人物。主角是最重要的角色。这个故事就是关于他们的。次要角色不如主要角色重要。 |  |
