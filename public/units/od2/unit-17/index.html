4 Grammar and Expression

Goal: match some, any, a few, and a little with count and non-count art nouns.
Count Nouns

We can count these nouns. They have singular and plural forms.

one circle → two circles

one shape → many shapes

Non-count Nouns

We usually do not count these materials one by one.

paper · glue · paint

Say a piece of paper or a bottle of glue.

a few / a little

Use a few with plural count nouns.

a few stars

Use a little with non-count nouns: a little glue.

Use some

Use some in affirmative sentences.

I have some circles.

We need some paper.

Use any

Use any in many questions and negative sentences.

Do you have any glue?

We don't have any paint.

Watch Out!Say a few shapes but a little glue. In this art lesson, say some paper, not some papers.
