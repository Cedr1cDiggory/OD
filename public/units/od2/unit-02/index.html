4 Grammar and Expression

Goal: use adverbs of frequency and pronouns to describe habits and animals.
Frequency Ladder: How Often?
100% alwaysusuallysometimeshardly ever0% never

Move from always to never: the action happens less and less often.

Before a Main Verb

Put the frequency word before an action verb.

Leo sometimes tries to catch Lily.

They never fight now.

After the Verb Be

With am, is, or are, put the frequency word after be.

Lily is usually nervous.

The birds are always noisy.

Match the Subject

The frequency word does not change, but the main verb may change.

I sometimes watch.

He sometimes watches.

Watch Out!never and hardly ever already have a negative meaning. Say They never fight., not They don't never fight.
Song Lines: Cats and Birds
My cat and bird always fight.
They never get along together.
I hardly ever sleep at night.
I usually have to shout.
I sometimes say they must be friends.
Pronoun Rule

Some words can take the place of nouns. These words are called pronouns.

Rabbits are very gentle. They are very gentle.
Pronoun Examples
