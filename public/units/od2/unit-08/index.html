2 Knowledge List Guide

Goal: retell the story with a beginning, a middle, and an end.
Reading Skill: BeginningThe beginning tells who is in the story and what starts the problem.
Reading Skill: Middle and EndThe middle shows the problem. The end tells how the story finishes.

Middle

4 Grammar and Expression

Goal: ask simple past questions with did and answer with clear story details.
Yes / No Pattern

Did + subject + base verb + ...?

Did Bandar steal food?

Did the people catch him?

Short Answers

Repeat did in a short answer.

Yes, he did.

No, he didn't.

Question-Word Pattern

Question word + did + subject + base verb + ...?

Where did Bandar hide?

Why did he run?

Statement → Question

Bandar stole bread.

→ Did Bandar steal bread?

Use the base verb after did.

Past Forms in Answers

Use past forms when you tell what happened.

steal → stole · run → ran

eat → ate · hide → hid

Watch Out!Say Did Bandar steal?, not Did Bandar stole?. Say He didn't hide., not He didn't hid.
