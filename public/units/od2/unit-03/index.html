2 Knowledge List Guide

Goal: use cause and effect to explain how matter changes when it gets hot or cold.
Reading Skill: CauseA cause tells why something happens. Ask: Why did it happen?
Reading Skill: EffectAn effect tells what happens after the cause. Ask: What happened next?
Three States

Everything in our world can be a solid, a liquid, or a gas.

Hot or Cold

Things can change from one state to another when we make them hot or cold.

Cause / Effect

Cause: We heat water. Effect: It boils and changes to steam.

4 Grammar and Expression

Goal: use was / were for past be, and choose made of or made from to talk about materials.

Key Expressions
be made ofbe made fromthink ofwas / wasn'twere / weren't
Use was

Use was with I, he, she, it, and one person or thing.

It was a liquid.

The ice was cold.

Use were

Use were with you, we, they, and plural nouns.

They were ice cubes.

The bags were wet.

Make It Negative

Add not after was or were. We often use the short forms.

was not → wasn't

were not → weren't

Talk About Change

Use past be for the old state and present be for the new state.

It was a liquid. Now it is a solid.

be made of

Use it when the material keeps its form and you can still recognize it.

The box is made of cardboard.

be made from

Use it when the original material changes into something new.

Steam is made from hot water.

Watch out: do not use didn't with past be. Say It wasn't a gas., not It didn't was a gas.

6 Writing Task

Choose one thing that can change state. Plan your answers, then write your paragraph on paper.

Writing goal: write 4-6 sentences about what it was, what you do to it, and what it is now.
Writing Prompt
Choose a thing that can change state and answer the questions about it.
What is it?Choose one thing, such as water, ice, snow, or an ice pop.
Is it a liquid / solid / gas?Name the first state.
What do you do to it?Heat it or make it cold.
What is it now?Name the new state after the change.
Model Paragraph

I choose water. It is a liquid. I make it very cold. Then it freezes and changes to ice. Now it is a solid. This is a cause-and-effect change.
