Use Picture Labels

Read the labels beside a picture. They explain important places and details.

Neighborhoods

Town or City

The Country

4 Grammar and Expression

Goal: notice the rule and say each example aloud.
One owner

Add 's: Jack's house; the farmer's field.

Plural owner

If a plural noun does not end in s, add 's: the children's school.

Shared ownership

Use 's after the second name: Ava and Ben's neighborhood.

6 Writing Task

Goal: read the model, plan your sentences, and write on paper.
Your Writing MissionWrite 4 sentences about where you live.Include: town, city, or country; who lives with you; what your home is like; things nearby.
Start: I live in ... with ... We have a ... home.Next: It is ... There are ... There are no ...
Model
I live in the country with my family. We have an old house. It is small. There are fields, trees, and animals. There are no big buildings.
