2 Knowledge List Guide

Goal: review the text clues and sort the ideas into wants, needs, helpers, money, and jobs.
Reading Skill: ReviewUse the title, headings, and pictures again. These clues help you retell what the text is about.
How to ReadAsk yourself: What do I see? What does the heading tell me? Is this something we want, or something we need?
Wants

Wants are things we would like to have.

Examples: chips, soda, and cookies at a picnic.

Needs

Needs are things we must have to live.

A home, food, water, and clothes are key examples in the text.

Helpers

Doctors, police officers, and teachers have service jobs.

When you read these words, think about how each person helps us.

3 Mind Map Workshop

Goal: explain what a want is, what a need is, and who helps us in everyday life.
WantsA want is something we would like to have, but it is not always something we must have to live.
NeedsA need helps us live: a home to live in, food and water to grow and stay healthy, and clothes to keep us warm.
Who do we need?Police officers keep us safe, doctors help keep us healthy, and teachers help us learn.
Money and JobsWe need money to buy things we need. People have jobs to get money.

4 Grammar and Expression

Goal: ask questions with Do / Does and What do / What does, and use to do to explain why we need something.
Yes / No Questions: be
If the sentence has a be verb, move be before the subject.
Are they doctors?
Is water a need?
Yes / No Questions: do / does
If there is no be verb, use an auxiliary verb before the subject.
Use Does with he / she / it. Use Do with other subjects.
What Questions
After What, use do or does, then the subject and the base verb.
What does + subject + base verb + ...?
What do + subject + base verb + ...?
need + to do
Use need + noun + to do to explain the reason.
We need a home to live in.
We need clothes to keep us warm.

6 Writing Task

Read the model paragraph. Then write about things you need. Use I need... and We use...to... to explain reasons.

Writing goal: write a 4-6 sentence paragraph about what you need, why you need it, and how it helps you.
Your Writing MissionWrite 4–6 sentences about things you need.Include: one need; what you use it to do; another need and its reason; one want that is different from a need.
Start: I need ... I use it to ...Next: We need ... to ... Then I want ...
Model Paragraph

We need money. We use money to buy food, clothes, and other things we need. Then we buy things we want!
