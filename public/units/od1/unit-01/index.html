Unit 1 · Families and Friends

Learn with pictures, short sentences, and touch activities.
Unit goal: Introduce family members and say how families help each other.

2 Knowledge List Guide

Goal: read the short cards and find the unit's big idea.
Predict from Pictures

Look at the people and actions. Who is in each family? What do they do together?

Big Idea

Families can be big or small. People in families help each other.

Useful Details

They live together, go places together, share things, and play together.

5 Challenge Practice

Goal: choose the best answer, then check your work.
Part A · Meet the Family

1. I ___ seven years old.

2. Mandy ___ eight years old.

3. They ___ cousins.

4. Her family ___ small.

Part B · Family Facts

5. My brother and I ___ good friends.

6. My grandmother ___ very helpful.

7. I ___ not my sister. I am her brother.

Choose one answer for each sentence.

6 Writing Task

Goal: read the model, plan your sentences, and write on paper.
Your Writing MissionWrite 4 sentences about you and your family.Include: your name and age; whether your family is big or small; who you live with; brothers or sisters.
Start: My name is ... I am ... years old.Next: I live with ... I have ...
Model
My name is Joe. I am seven years old. My family is big. I live with my mother and father. I have two brothers and two sisters.
Line 1My name is ____. I am ____ years old.
Line 2My family is big / small.
Line 3I live with my ____.
Line 4I have ____.
Write your final work by hand on paper. Check capital letters and punctuation, then give it to your teacher.
