4 Grammar and Expression

Goal: notice the rule and say each example aloud.
Where is ...?

Use is with one thing: Where is the nest?

Where are ...?

Use are with more than one: Where are the chicks?

Plural nouns

nest → nests, chick → chicks, baby → babies, child → children

Goal: choose the best answer, then check your work.

6 Writing Task

Goal: read the model, plan your sentences, and write on paper.
Your Writing MissionWrite 4 sentences about one animal and its home.Include: the animal; where it lives; what it eats; how the home keeps it safe.
Start: My animal is a ... It lives in ...Next: It eats ... Its home keeps it ...
Model
My animal is an eagle. It lives in a nest. The nest keeps the eagle safe.

Write your final paragraph by hand on paper. Check capital letters and punctuation, then give it to your teacher.
