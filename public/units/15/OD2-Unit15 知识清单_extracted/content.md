| 短语 | in many ways在很多方面<br>the cause of …的原因<br>the effect of …的影响 | 短语会造句 |
| 阅读技巧 | Remember, a cause is why something happens.<br>The effect is what happens after the cause.<br>记住，cause是指某事发生的原因。结果是在原因之后发生的。 |  |
| 重点句型 | We see people walking, birds flying, and leaves falling to the ground on a windy day. 在刮风的日子里，我们看到人们在散步，鸟儿在飞翔，树叶落在地上。<br>Things move in many ways.事物以多种方式运动。<br>They move up and down, right and left, and over and under things.它们上下左右移动，在物体上面和下面移动。<br>A push is a force, and it moves something away from you. 推力是一种力，它使某物远离你。<br>We push people on swings.我们推着人们荡秋千。<br>A pull is a force, too, and it moves something toward you. 拉力也是一种力，它使某物向你移动。<br>Can you name something you push and something you pull? 你能说出你推的和拉的东西吗?<br>It is easier to move a light thing than a heavy thing. 搬动轻的东西比搬动重的东西容易。<br>We can move a toy car with a small force because it's light. 我们可以用很小的力移动玩具车，因为它很轻。<br>We need a big force to move a real car because it's heavy.我们需要很大的力来移动一辆真车，因为它很重。<br>What's the cause of the door opening slowly? 门开得慢的原因是什么?<br>What's the effect of the door being pushed with a small force?用很小的力推门会有什么效果. |  |
