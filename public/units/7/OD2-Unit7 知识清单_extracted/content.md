| 阅读技巧 | To reread means to read a text again. You can reread something for many different reasons, such as when you don't understand something. When you reread, read slowly and think about what you're reading.<br>重读是指再读一篇文章。你可以因为很多不同的原因重读一些东西，比如当你不理解一些东西的时候。当你重读的时候，慢慢读，思考你正在读的内容。 |  |
| 重点句型 | When we add things, we join them together. 当我们添加东西时，我们把它们连接在一起。<br>When we subtract things, we take them away from other things. 当我们做减法的时候，我们把一个数从另一个数中减去。<br>Then we can see how many things are left.然后我们可以看到还剩下多少东西。<br>When we count backward, we're subtracting 1 each time. 当我们向后数的时候，每次都减去1。<br>Count backward from 50 to 1. 从50倒数到1。<br>Now count backward by fives subtracting 5 each time. 现在向后数，每次5减去5。<br>Now count backward by tens.现在按十倒数。<br>How much are you subtracting each time now?现在每次要减去多少?<br>The minus sign tells us to subtract 4 from 9.负号告诉我们9减去4。<br>Nine minus four equals five. 九减四等于五。<br>Now think of some things that people subtract every day.现在想想人们每天都要减法的东西。<br>My teacher subtracts numbers from 100 for my test score! 我的老师用100减去数字来计算我的考试成绩。 |  |
