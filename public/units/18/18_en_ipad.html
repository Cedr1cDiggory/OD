4 Grammar and Expression

Goal: ask about quantity with how many and how much.
How Many

Use how many with count nouns. Example: How many cranes?

How Much

Use how much with non-count nouns. Example: How much glue?

Answer

Use numbers or quantity words: a lot of paper, a few cranes, a little glue.
