| 重点句型 | Living things need clean air to breathe and clean water to drink.生物需要呼吸干净的空气，喝干净的水。<br>We shouldn't hurt our natural resources because we can't usually make more of them.我们不应该伤害我们的自然资源，因为我们通常不能制造更多的资源。<br>If the air is dirty, people can get sick and plants can't grow. 如果空气脏了，人们就会生病，植物就不能生长。<br>Trash is very bad for our natural resources.垃圾对我们的自然资源非常有害。<br>People can't use that land, and farmers can't grow food on it.人们不能使用这些土地，农民也不能在上面种植粮食。<br>We can all take care of the Earth's resources. 我们都可以照顾地球的资源。<br>Use things until you can't use them anymore.使用一些东西，直到你再也不能使用为止。<br>Don't throw things away. 不要乱扔东西。<br>Reuse them. 重用它们。<br>Refill plastic drink bottles.重新填充塑料饮料瓶。<br>Make toys from old things.用旧东西做玩具。<br>Reduce the natural resources you use. 减少自然资源的使用。<br>Take your own bag to the store.把你自己的包带到商店去。 |  |
