## 1 Key Vocabulary

Goal: Learn animal words and actions.

Words:

- worm
- berries
- hunt
- escape
- creep
- fight
- peck
- pinecone
- squawk

Sentence practice:

1. Birds peck at their food to eat it.
2. A pinecone grows on a pine tree.
3. Strawberries are my favorite kind of berries.
4. Some animals in the rainforest hunt for their food.
5. The dog and the cat do not like each other. They often fight.
6. A worm is a long, thin animal. It lives underground.
7. Bobby's pet bird can escape out of the window.
8. Cats can creep very quietly. Other animals cannot hear them coming.
9. Birds can squawk loudly. It is a funny sound.

## 2 Before You Read

Goal: Use compare and contrast before reading.

Think:

- How can animals help people?
- Do you help at home?

Reading strategy:

- To compare things, tell how they are the same.
- To contrast things, tell how they are different.

Practice text:

Hugo and Ivan are ten years old. Hugo lives in a big house in the country and Ivan lives in a small apartment in the city. They both have pets. Hugo has a big dog and Ivan has a small goldfish.

Venn diagram notes:

- Hugo: big house, country, big dog
- Both: ten years old, have pets
- Ivan: small apartment, city, small goldfish

Story preview:

- Title: Leo and Lily's Adventure
- Characters: Leo the cat and Lily the bird
- Genre: adventure story
- Preview idea: adventure stories are exciting because many things happen.

## 3 Reading Comprehension

Goal: Talk about favorite parts, compare characters, and order events.

Favorite part prompts:

- Why Leo and Lily do not like each other
- Their adventure in the woods
- How Leo escapes from the fox
- Why Leo and Lily are friends at the end

Speaking prompt:

- What's your favorite part?
- Example answer: Lily pecks the pinecone. It's funny!

Compare and contrast Leo and Lily:

- Word bank: can fly, scared, clever, can run, friendly
- Use a Venn diagram with Leo / Both / Lily.

Event order:

1. Leo chases Lily.
2. Leo and Lily are lost.
3. Leo tries to fight the fox.
4. Leo and Lily wait in a tree.
5. Lily shows Leo the way home.
6. Leo carries Lily home.

Think:

- Which animals can be pets?
- Can animals help each other?

## 4 Grammar in Use: Adverbs of Frequency

Goal: Use adverbs of frequency to talk about habits and actions.

Song: Cats and Birds

Key lines:

- My cat and bird always fight.
- They never get along together.
- I hardly ever sleep at night.
- I usually have to shout.
- I sometimes say they must be friends.

Grammar:

- Lily always escapes.
- Lily is usually very nervous.
- Leo sometimes tries to catch Lily.
- Leo hardly ever tries to catch Lily now.
- Leo and Lily never fight now.

Frequency words:

- always
- usually
- sometimes
- hardly ever
- never

Partner practice:

- I always ...
- I usually ...
- I sometimes ...
- I hardly ever ...
- I never ...
- He / She always ...
- He / She usually ...
- He / She sometimes ...
- He / She hardly ever ...
- He / She never ...

Speaking examples:

- I always eat ice cream in the summer.
- He sometimes watches TV in the evening.

## 5 Communicate

Goal: Describe animals with adjectives.

Words:

- strong
- gentle
- patient
- smart
- calm
- fierce

True or False practice:

1. An elephant can be big and strong.
2. A lion is always gentle and calm.
3. A dog can be fierce and patient.
4. A monkey can be smart.
5. A mouse is always strong and fierce.

Listening prompts:

- What animals do you think are smart? Why?
- Listen. Where are the children?
- Listen again and check the correct animal.

## 6 Speaking

Goal: Describe an animal and name its animal group.

Model:

- Eagles have feathers and wings to help them fly. What animal group are they in?
- Eagles are in the bird group.

Useful sentence frames:

- These animals have ...
- They can ...
- They're in ...

## 7 Word Study: Pronouns

Goal: Use pronouns to replace nouns.

Rule:

- Some words can take the place of nouns.
- These words are called pronouns.

Example:

- Rabbits are very gentle.
- They are very gentle.

Practice:

1. My friends and I are playing. -> We
2. The dog is barking at Tom. -> It / He
3. The drums are very loud. -> They
4. The city is dangerous. -> It
5. Lions can be fierce. -> They

## 8 Writing

Task:

- Tell your partner about an animal that you know.
- Say what it is like.

Paper-writing reminder:

- Do not write the full paragraph on the webpage.

## 10 Project: Make Animal Picture Cards

Goal: Make and share Animal Picture Cards.

Steps:

1. Draw or find a picture of one animal for each card.
2. Write the name of the animal below the picture.
3. Write three facts about the animal group on the back of the card.
4. Put the Animal Picture Cards on the wall.
5. Tell the class about an animal.
6. Play a guessing game with partners. Put the cards picture-side down. Partners read the facts and guess the animals.

Example:

- This is a rabbit.
- Rabbits have fur all over their bodies.

Back-of-card example:

- Mammal Group
- Mammals have fur or hair covering their bodies.
- Most mammals have two or four legs.
- Mammal babies drink milk from their mothers.
