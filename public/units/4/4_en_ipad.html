2 Knowledge List Guide

Goal: use cause and effect to explain why the ice cream melted and how the class made more.
Reading Skill: CauseA cause tells why something happens. Ask: Why did it happen?
Reading Skill: EffectAn effect tells what happens after the cause. Ask: What happened next?
Problem

The freezer door was open all night, so the ice cream melted.

Solution

Use cream, sugar, vanilla, ice, salt, and bags to make new ice cream.

Retell Tip

Say the cause first, then say the effect: The door was open, so the ice cream melted.

3 Mind Map Workshop

Goal: retell the ice cream story with the problem, the ingredients, and the steps.

Problem

The freezer door was open all night. The ice cream melted.

Ingredients

Cream, sugar, vanilla, ice cubes, and salt help make ice cream.

Steps

Pour the mixture into a small bag. Put ice and salt into a big bag.

4 Grammar and Expression

Goal: ask questions with was / were and answer in the simple past.
Was Questions

Use was with I / he / she / it. Example: Was the freezer door open?

Were Questions

Use were with you / we / they. Example: Were the games fun?

Short Answers

Yes, it was. No, it wasn't. Yes, they were. No, they weren't.
