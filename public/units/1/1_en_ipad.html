2 Knowledge List Guide

Goal: review the text clues and sort the ideas in your mind by animal group, body covering, movement, and babies.
Reading Skill: CompareTo compare things, tell how they are the same. Birds and fish both lay eggs.
Reading Skill: ContrastTo contrast things, tell how they are different. Birds have feathers, but fish have scales and gills.

Animal Groups

Each group has special body parts.

Body Covering

Babies

3 Mind Map Workshop

Goal: use the mind map to explain how birds, fish, amphibians, and mammals are amazing in different ways.

Retell

Animals can breathe and move, but they are different in many ways.

Compare

Birds, fish, and amphibians lay eggs. Mammal babies drink milk.

Contrast

Birds use wings. Fish use gills and fins. Frogs use legs to jump on land.

4 Grammar and Expression

Goal: use subject and object pronouns to make animal sentences shorter and clearer.
Rule Cards
Subject

Use before the verb.

They have wings.

Object

Use after the verb.

Look at them.

One Animal

Use it for one animal or one thing.

It has gills.
